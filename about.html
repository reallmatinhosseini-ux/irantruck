A family business serving Iran’s heavy-vehicle industry.

For more than 25 years, Iran Truck Trading Group has supplied the parts that keep trucks and commercial vehicles moving.

01 — Who we are

A trusted commercial group based in Mashhad and the official representative of LEO in Iran. We specialise in parts for heavy trucks, machinery and commercial vehicles — and in the relationships that come with them.

Quality, availability and people who know the trade.

Our commitment to quality, availability and professional service has made us a recognised name in Iran’s transportation industry.
