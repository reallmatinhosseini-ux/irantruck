Who we are

Built on expertise and lasting relationships

Iran Truck Trading Group is a trusted commercial group based in Mashhad and the official representative of the LEO brand in Iran. We specialise in parts for heavy trucks, machinery and commercial vehicles.

Our commitment to quality, availability and professional service has made us a recognised name in Iran’s transportation industry. We serve fleet owners, workshops and logistics companies across the country — and we stay with them long after the sale.
